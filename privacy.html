Privacy Policy for YASNO Weather

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how YASNO Weather handles information on your device.

1. Location Data

YASNO Weather uses your device's location to instantly show the weather forecast for your current city. Your geographical coordinates are processed strictly on your local device and are never transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. You can also opt to enter your city manually without providing location access.

2. Data Collection and Third Parties

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data, usage statistics, or device identifiers. The application operates entirely without user accounts or tracking scripts.

3. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
