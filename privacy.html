Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026
TL;DR — Yasno has no own backend or analytics. Preferences, consent, one last selected place and one last forecast are stored locally. Coordinates and city searches go directly to external services.

1. Introduction

Yasno is a free weather application developed and operated by Ivan Suslov. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes and how.

By using Yasno you agree to the terms described below. If you do not agree — please do not use the app.

2. Data Yasno does not keep

Yasno does not keep personal profiles or histories of your activity. It has no own backend or analytics. Requests sent directly to external providers are described in section 4.

  • Your name, email, phone number or any identity
  • Your location history
  • Your search queries for cities
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, fingerprints
  • Usage statistics or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Location data

If you allow geolocation, coordinates are sent directly from your device to Open-Meteo for a forecast and to Nominatim for reverse geocoding.

  • Coordinates are sent directly from your device to the weather API (Open-Meteo) — they never pass through our servers.
  • Yasno does not keep a location history; it stores only the last selected place locally, as described in section 5.
  • You can always disable location in your device settings or type the city manually.

4. Weather data

Forecasts are requested from Open-Meteo. City search and reverse geocoding use OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Font files are requested from Google Fonts.

Open-Meteo receives coordinates for forecasts; Nominatim receives coordinates for reverse geocoding and the place names you search. Google receives font requests. These providers may also receive your IP address and standard connection data. Yasno has no own backend or analytics.

5. Local storage

Yasno stores these items locally on your device:

  • Selected language
  • Temperature unit (°C / °F)
  • The last selected place, including its coordinates, and one last forecast with its original receive time, for reuse and offline viewing
  • Your consent to this policy and whether the location prompt has been shown

These items are stored locally on your device. Requests described in section 4 go directly to the named providers. The service worker does not cache API responses. Offline, Yasno shows only the last forecast it received, with its original receive time in the Updated field. Clear app data to delete the local items.

6. Language translations

Yasno includes hand-written interface translations for 13 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Indonesian, Arabic and Turkish.

Yasno does not use Google Translate or any other automatic translation service for the app interface or this policy. Only the translations bundled with the app are used.

7. Children

Yasno does not ask for your age. The data practices described above apply to all users. Age ratings may vary by region.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the new version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer, Ivan Suslov, at yasno.weather@proton.me.
