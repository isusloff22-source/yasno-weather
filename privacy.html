Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026
TL;DR — Yasno does not collect, store or share your personal data. Everything works on your device.

1. Introduction

Yasno ("we", "the app") is a free weather application that works worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes and how.

By using Yasno you agree to the terms described below. If you do not agree — please do not use the app.

2. We do not collect personal data

Yasno is built with privacy as the top priority. We do not collect, do not store on any server and do not share with anyone:

  • Your name, email, phone number or any identity
  • Your location history
  • Your search queries for cities
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, fingerprints
  • Usage statistics or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no trackers and no third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Location data

If you allow geolocation, your device's coordinates are used only to fetch the weather for your current city.

  • Coordinates are sent directly from your device to the weather API (Open-Meteo) — they never pass through our servers.
  • We do not save your location anywhere — not on a server, not in analytics.
  • You can always disable location in your browser settings or type the city manually.

4. Weather data

Forecast is loaded from the free open service Open-Meteo. Geocoding (city search) uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

These services see only the coordinates or the city name you are searching for. They do not receive any identifier tied to you personally.

5. Local storage (cookies)

Yasno stores only in the browser's localStorage on your device:

  • Selected language
  • Temperature unit (°C / °F)
  • Last searched city — to open it again next time
  • Your consent to this Privacy Policy

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

6. Automatic translation

For languages that do not have a hand-written translation, Yasno may use Google Translate API to translate interface strings.

Only short UI labels are sent for translation — never your search queries, location or any user data.

7. Children

Yasno does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the new version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue in the project's GitHub repository — link is in the footer of the app.
